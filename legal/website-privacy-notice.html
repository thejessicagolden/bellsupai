Website Privacy Notice

Version 1.0 · Effective March 10, 2026  ·  bellsup.ai

Key Points

This notice covers the bellsup.ai marketing website only. If you use the Bells Up AI platform at app.bellsup.ai, your use is governed by our separate Platform Privacy Policy.

We collect minimal data. We do not use analytics, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party monitoring tools on this website.

We do not sell your data. We have no advertising relationships that involve visitor data.

01

What We Collect and Why

Server Logs

When you visit the Site, our hosting infrastructure automatically processes your IP address, browser type (user agent), the pages you requested, the referring URL, and the date and time of each request. This data is a technical necessity of serving web pages and is used solely for security monitoring and troubleshooting.

Waitlist and Contact Form Submissions

If you submit a form on the Site, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, email address, firm or company name, and any message text. Form submissions are processed by Formspree, a third-party form handling service, and delivered to us via email.

We use this information to respond to your inquiry and, if you have requested access, to contact you when access is available. We do not add you to marketing lists unless you have specifically opted in.

Do not submit privileged, confidential, or sensitive client information through website forms. Form submissions are delivered by email and are not subject to the end-to-end encryption protections that apply within the platform.

What We Do Not Collect

We do not use analytics tools, advertising pixels, social media tracking, or any third-party monitoring technologies on this website. We do not set cookies. We do not build visitor profiles or track your browsing behavior across sites.

02

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we process personal data under the following legal bases:

Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): Server log processing is necessary for the security and operation of the website. Our legitimate interest is in maintaining a secure, functioning website. The data involved is limited to standard technical metadata.

Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): When you submit a waitlist or contact form, you consent to our processing of the information you provide. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

03

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The bellsup.ai marketing website does not set cookies. We do not use local storage, session storage, or any other client-side tracking mechanism.

The Bells Up AI platform at app.bellsup.ai uses a single functional authentication cookie, described in the Platform Privacy Policy. That cookie is scoped to app.bellsup.ai and is not set or read by this website.

08

Your Rights

All Visitors

You may contact us at privacy@bellsup.ai to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. If you submitted a waitlist or contact form, you may request removal at any time. We will respond within 30 days.

US State Privacy Rights

California (CCPA/CPRA) and other US states: Bells Up AI does not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. These statements apply as those terms are defined under applicable state privacy laws.

Categories of personal information we collect: Identifiers (name, email, IP address) and internet or electronic network activity (server logs).

Your rights: Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Because we do not sell or share personal information, there is no sale or sharing to opt out of.

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@bellsup.ai. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Additional Rights Under GDPR (EEA / UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection regarding your personal data. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In Portugal, this is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD) at www.cnpd.pt.

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@bellsup.ai.
