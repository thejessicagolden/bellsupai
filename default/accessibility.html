Accessibility Statement

Version 1.0 · Effective April 15, 2026  ·  bellsup.ai

Bells Up AI is committed to making our product accessible to everyone, including users of assistive technology. We design and build to conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at the AA level, and to the AAA level where possible.

WCAG 2.1 addresses accessibility across a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological, and applies across desktops, mobile devices, and other platforms.

Our approach

WCAG 2.1 provides a strong baseline, but we recognize that guidelines alone don't address every user need. We go beyond minimum requirements where we can, and we validate our conformance claims through ongoing testing. That validation process is continuous, not a one-time exercise.

Known limitations

Our product is currently unstable on mobile operating systems. We are focused first on verifying WCAG 2.1 AA conformance on desktop, and will be improving mobile stability soon.

Contact us

If you have questions or would like to report an accessibility issue with Bells Up AI or our website, please email info@bellsup.ai. Our point of contact for accessibility is Marc Seawright.
